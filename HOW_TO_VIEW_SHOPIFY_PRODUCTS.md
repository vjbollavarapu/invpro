# How to View Your Synced Shopify Products

## ✅ **IMMEDIATE ANSWER - 2 Ways to View:**

### **Option 1: View Synced Products (Before Import)**
**Location:** Settings → Integrations → Shopify → **"Products" tab**
- Shows all products synced from Shopify (currently 17 products)
- This is the integration table view

### **Option 2: View in Inventory (After Import)**
**Location:** Dashboard → **Inventory** page
- Shows products imported to your main inventory
- Products appear here AFTER they're imported

## 🚀 **To See Products in Inventory Page:**

**Just click "Sync Products" again** - it will automatically import them now!

1. Go to: **Settings → Integrations → Shopify → Sync tab**
2. Click: **"Sync Products"** button
3. Wait for sync to complete
4. Go to: **Inventory page** - your products will be there!
